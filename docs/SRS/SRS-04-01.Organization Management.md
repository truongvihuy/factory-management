# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

This document specifies the functional requirements for the **Organization Management** module.

The Organization Management module is responsible for maintaining the hierarchical structure of an enterprise, including Companies, Factories, Workshops, and Production Lines.

This hierarchy serves as the foundation for all business entities within the Factory Management System.

---

# 2. Business Scope

The module supports management of:

- Company
- Factory
- Workshop
- Production Line

The module does **not** manage production planning or manufacturing execution.

---

# 3. Organization Hierarchy

```text
Company
    │
    ├── Factory
            │
            ├── Workshop
                    │
                    ├── Production Line
```

Business Rules

- One Company owns multiple Factories.
- One Factory belongs to one Company.
- One Factory contains multiple Workshops.
- One Workshop belongs to one Factory.
- One Workshop contains multiple Production Lines.
- One Production Line belongs to one Workshop.

# 4. Functional Requirements

---

# FR-ORG-001 Create Company

## Description

The system shall allow an administrator to create a Company.

---

### Actors

- System Administrator

---

### Priority

High

---

### Business Rules

- BR-001

---

### Preconditions

Administrator is authenticated.

---

### Trigger

Administrator selects **Create Company**.

---

### Main Flow

1. Open Create Company page.
2. Enter Company information.
3. Validate input.
4. Save Company.
5. Display success message.

---

### Alternative Flow

None.

---

### Exception Flow

- Duplicate Company Code.
- Missing required fields.

---

### Postconditions

A new Company is created.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

- Company Code must be unique.
- Company Name is required.

---

# FR-ORG-002 Update Company

## Description

The system shall allow updating Company information.

---

### Actors

- System Administrator

---

### Business Rules

- BR-001

---

### Preconditions

---

### Main Flow

1. Search Company.
2. Edit information.
3. Save.
4. Record audit log.

---

### Exception Flow

Company not found.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

# FR-ORG-003 Disable Company

## Description

Companies cannot be physically deleted.

The system shall support disabling a Company.

---

### Business Rules

Historical data shall remain available.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

Disabled Companies cannot receive new Factories.

# 6. Non-functional Considerations

- Organization hierarchy should load within **2 seconds** under normal conditions.
- All organization changes shall be recorded in the audit log.
- Historical relationships shall never be removed.
- Soft delete shall be used instead of physical deletion.
- Only authorized users may modify organization data.
