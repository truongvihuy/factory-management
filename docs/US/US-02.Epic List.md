# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Epics of the Factory Management System (FMS).

An **Epic** represents a large business capability that delivers significant business value and is composed of multiple Features and User Stories.

This document serves as the top-level decomposition of the Product Backlog and provides traceability between business requirements, functional requirements, and implementation activities.

# 5. Epic Descriptions

## EP-01 Authentication & Authorization

Provide secure user authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), and factory-level authorization.

---

## EP-02 Organization Management

Manage organizations (companies) within the system, including organization lifecycle and hierarchy.

---

## EP-03 Factory Management

Manage factories, workshops, and production lines.

Maintain historical records of organizational structure changes.

---

## EP-04 Machine Management

Manage industrial machines throughout their lifecycle, including registration, relocation, operational status, maintenance history, and production line assignment.

---

## EP-05 Sensor Management

Manage sensors attached to machines.

Maintain complete sensor assignment history when sensors are transferred between machines.

---

## EP-06 Gateway Management

Manage industrial gateways responsible for collecting telemetry data from field devices through MQTT, OPC-UA, and Modbus TCP.

---

## EP-07 Telemetry Management

Collect, validate, process, store, archive, and query real-time telemetry generated by industrial equipment.

---

## EP-08 Alert Management

Detect abnormal operating conditions based on configurable thresholds.

Generate alerts, escalation workflows, acknowledgements, and notifications.

---

## EP-09 Maintenance Management

Support preventive, corrective, and predictive maintenance.

Manage maintenance plans, work orders, checklists, approvals, engineers, and maintenance history.

---

## EP-10 Incident Management

Manage equipment incidents throughout the entire incident lifecycle, including SLA monitoring and Major Incident support.

---

## EP-11 Dashboard

Provide real-time operational dashboards displaying machine status, alerts, KPIs, maintenance status, and production health.

---

## EP-12 Reporting

Provide operational reports, KPI reports, historical analysis, PDF export, Excel export, and scheduled reporting.

---

## EP-13 Notification Management

Deliver notifications through Dashboard, Email, Mobile Push Notification, and SMS for critical events.

---

## EP-14 File & Attachment Management

Manage document attachments including maintenance documents, incident evidence, manuals, inspection reports, and machine images.

---

## EP-15 Audit Log Management

Record all significant user and system activities for traceability and compliance.

---

## EP-16 Master Data Management

Manage shared reference data including:

- Machine Types
- Manufacturers
- Incident Categories
- Maintenance Types
- Alert Types
- Units of Measurement
- Status Codes

---

## EP-17 Spare Part Inventory Management

Manage spare part inventory, suppliers, stock movement, reservation, consumption, and replacement history.

---

## EP-18 System Configuration Management

Manage configurable system settings including alert thresholds, retention policies, notification rules, and scheduler configuration.

---

## EP-19 Data Archive & Retention Management

Archive historical operational data according to retention policies while preserving long-term reporting capabilities.

---

## EP-20 API & Integration Management

Provide secure APIs and external integrations with ERP systems, MES systems, mobile applications, and third-party services.

---

## EP-21 Background Job & Scheduler Management

Execute scheduled background processes such as maintenance generation, SLA monitoring, report generation, notification retry, telemetry aggregation, and archive jobs.

Each Epic shall be decomposed into:

```text
Epic
    ↓
Feature
    ↓
User Story
    ↓
Acceptance Criteria
    ↓
Development Task
    ↓
Test Case
```

Each Epic shall maintain traceability to:

- BRD
- SRS
- Business Rules
- Use Cases
- API Specifications
- Database Design
- Test Cases
