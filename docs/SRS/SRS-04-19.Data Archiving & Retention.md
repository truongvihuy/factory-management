# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements for the **Data Archiving & Retention Management** module.

The Data Archiving & Retention Management module is responsible for managing the lifecycle of historical business and telemetry data. It ensures operational databases remain performant while preserving historical records for reporting, analytics, compliance, and auditing.

Archived data remains accessible according to user permissions but is separated from active operational data.

---

# 2. Business Scope

The module supports:

- Data Retention Policy
- Automatic Data Archiving
- Archive Monitoring
- Archive Search
- Archive Restore
- Archive Verification
- Archive Reporting
- Archive Audit Logging

---

# 3. Supported Archive Objects

The system shall support archiving the following data.

| Data Type | Retention Policy |
|------------|-----------------|
| Raw Telemetry | 6 Months |
| Aggregated Telemetry | 5 Years |
| Alerts | Configurable |
| Maintenance Work Orders | Configurable |
| Incident Tickets | Configurable |
| Audit Logs | Configurable |
| Notification History | Configurable |
| Dashboard Snapshots (Future) | Configurable |

Retention periods shall be configurable through the **System Configuration Management** module.

---

# 4. Business Rules

- Active and archived data shall be logically separated.
- Archived data shall remain searchable.
- Archived data shall be read-only.
- Archived data shall preserve original timestamps.
- Historical relationships between business entities shall remain intact.
- Archive operations shall not interrupt normal system operation.

---

# 5. Functional Requirements

---

# FR-ARC-001 Configure Retention Policy

The system shall support configurable retention policies for each supported data type.

Retention policies shall define:

- Data Type
- Retention Period
- Archive Schedule
- Archive Destination

---

# FR-ARC-002 Automatic Archiving

The system shall automatically archive expired data according to configured retention policies.

Archive jobs shall execute on scheduled intervals.

---

# FR-ARC-003 Manual Archive

Authorized administrators may manually trigger archive operations.

Manual archive jobs shall follow the same validation rules as scheduled jobs.

---

# FR-ARC-004 Archive Monitoring

The system shall display archive job status including:

- Running
- Completed
- Failed
- Cancelled

Each archive job shall include execution statistics.

---

# FR-ARC-005 Archive Search

Authorized users shall search archived data using:

- Company
- Factory
- Module
- Entity Type
- Time Range

Support sorting and pagination.

---

# FR-ARC-006 Archive Restore

Authorized administrators may restore archived records.

Restored records shall:

- Preserve original identifiers.
- Preserve historical timestamps.
- Maintain business relationships.

All restore operations shall be audited.

---

# FR-ARC-007 Archive Verification

The system shall verify archive integrity before removing active records.

Verification includes:

- Record Count
- Checksum (Optional)
- Data Consistency

Failed verification shall cancel the archive operation.

---

# FR-ARC-008 Archive Report

Generate archive reports including:

- Archived Records
- Archive Size
- Execution Duration
- Failed Jobs
- Storage Utilization

---

# FR-ARC-009 Archive Dashboard

Display:

- Total Archived Records
- Archive Growth
- Upcoming Archive Jobs
- Failed Archive Jobs

---

# FR-ARC-010 Archive Notification

The system shall generate notifications when:

- Archive Job Completed
- Archive Job Failed
- Archive Storage Reaches Threshold

Notifications shall be sent through the Notification Management module.

---

# FR-ARC-011 Archive History

Maintain archive execution history including:

- Job ID
- Start Time
- End Time
- Status
- Operator
- Archived Record Count

---

# FR-ARC-012 Archive Authorization

Only authorized administrators may:

- Configure retention policies.
- Execute archive jobs.
- Restore archived data.

---

# FR-ARC-013 Archive Audit Logging

The system shall record:

- Archive Started
- Archive Completed
- Archive Failed
- Archive Restored
- Retention Policy Updated

Each audit record shall include:

- User
- Timestamp
- Action
- Result

# 7. Non-functional Considerations

## Performance

- Archive operations shall execute without significantly impacting online users.
- Archive searches should complete within **5 seconds**.
- Restore operations should complete within an acceptable time based on data volume.

---

## Reliability

- Archive operations shall be transactional.
- Active records shall not be removed until archive verification succeeds.
- Archive failures shall not result in data loss.

---

## Availability

- Archive services should achieve **99.9% availability**.
- Scheduled archive jobs should support automatic retry.

---

## Security

- Archived data shall follow the same authorization model as active data.
- Archived records shall be read-only.
- Restore operations shall require administrative privileges.

---

## Scalability

The module shall support:

- Billions of telemetry records.
- Multi-year historical storage.
- Multi-company deployments.
- Independent scaling of archive storage.

---

## Data Integrity

- Archived data shall preserve all business relationships.
- Original timestamps shall remain unchanged.
- Archived records shall remain immutable.
- Archive history shall be permanently retained.
