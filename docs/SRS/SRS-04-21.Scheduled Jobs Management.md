# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements for the **Background Job & Scheduler Management** module.

The Background Job & Scheduler Management module is responsible for executing scheduled and asynchronous system tasks without user interaction. It ensures that recurring operational processes are executed reliably, monitored continuously, and recover automatically in case of failures.

This module is a shared platform service used by multiple business modules.

---

# 2. Business Scope

The module supports:

- Scheduled Jobs
- Background Jobs
- Job Queue
- Job Execution
- Job Retry
- Job Monitoring
- Job History
- Job Configuration
- Audit Logging

---

# 3. Business Scenarios

The scheduler shall support recurring system tasks including:

| Module | Scheduled Job |
|----------|---------------|
| Maintenance | Generate preventive maintenance work orders |
| Notification | Retry failed notifications |
| Alert | Escalate overdue alerts |
| Incident | SLA monitoring |
| Telemetry | Aggregate telemetry data |
| Archive | Archive expired records |
| Reporting | Generate scheduled reports |
| Gateway | Gateway heartbeat monitoring |
| Dashboard | Refresh summary cache |
| System | Cleanup temporary files |

Future scheduled jobs may be added without affecting existing jobs.

---

# 4. Functional Requirements

---

# FR-JOB-001 Create Scheduled Job

Authorized administrators shall create scheduled jobs.

Required information:

- Job Name
- Job Type
- Schedule
- Status
- Description

---

# FR-JOB-002 Update Scheduled Job

Authorized administrators may update:

- Schedule
- Status
- Retry Policy
- Timeout
- Description

Changes shall not affect completed job history.

---

# FR-JOB-003 Enable / Disable Job

Jobs may be:

- Enabled
- Disabled

Disabled jobs shall not be executed until re-enabled.

---

# FR-JOB-004 Execute Scheduled Job

The system shall automatically execute jobs based on their configured schedules.

Supported schedule types include:

- Every Minute
- Hourly
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Cron Expression

---

# FR-JOB-005 Execute Manual Job

Authorized administrators may manually trigger job execution.

Manual executions shall be recorded separately from scheduled executions.

---

# FR-JOB-006 Job Queue

The system shall maintain a queue of pending jobs.

Queued jobs shall include:

- Pending
- Running
- Completed
- Failed
- Cancelled

---

# FR-JOB-007 Retry Failed Job

Failed jobs shall be retried automatically according to configured retry policies.

Retry attempts shall be recorded.

---

# FR-JOB-008 Job Monitoring

Display:

- Running Jobs
- Completed Jobs
- Failed Jobs
- Queue Length
- Average Execution Time

---

# FR-JOB-009 Job History

Maintain execution history including:

- Job Name
- Execution Time
- Duration
- Status
- Trigger Type
- Error Message

---

# FR-JOB-010 Job Notification

The system shall generate notifications for:

- Repeated Job Failures
- Long-running Jobs
- Disabled Critical Jobs

Notifications shall use the Notification Management module.

---

# FR-JOB-011 Job Configuration

Administrators shall configure:

- Retry Count
- Retry Interval
- Timeout
- Concurrency Limit
- Priority

---

# FR-JOB-012 Scheduled Report

The scheduler shall support automatic report generation.

Generated reports may be:

- Stored
- Emailed
- Downloaded

---

# FR-JOB-013 Archive Scheduler

The scheduler shall execute archive jobs according to configured retention policies.

Archive execution history shall be maintained.

---

# FR-JOB-014 Health Check

The system shall monitor scheduler health.

Failures shall trigger alerts.

---

# FR-JOB-015 Audit Logging

The system shall record:

- Job Created
- Job Updated
- Job Enabled
- Job Disabled
- Manual Execution
- Configuration Changed

Each audit record shall include:

- User
- Timestamp
- Action
- Result

# 6. Non-functional Considerations

## Performance

- Scheduled jobs shall execute at the configured time with minimal delay.
- Job execution shall not significantly impact interactive user operations.

---

## Reliability

- Failed jobs shall support automatic retry.
- Job execution shall be idempotent where applicable.
- Job history shall remain permanently traceable.

---

## Availability

- Scheduler services should achieve **99.9% availability**.

---

## Security

- Only authorized administrators may modify scheduler configurations.
- Manual execution shall require appropriate permissions.
- All scheduler operations shall be audited.

---

## Scalability

The scheduler shall support:

- Thousands of scheduled jobs.
- Concurrent job execution.
- Distributed execution across multiple application instances.

---

## Data Integrity

- Each scheduled execution shall have a unique execution record.
- Job history shall be immutable.
- Duplicate execution of critical jobs should be prevented whenever possible.
