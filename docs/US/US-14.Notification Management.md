# 5. Business Rules

| Rule ID | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| BR-100 | Notifications are generated only from supported system events. |
| BR-101 | Users receive notifications only for factories they are authorized to access. |
| BR-102 | SMS notifications are reserved for Critical events. |
| BR-103 | Escalation notifications follow configured SLA rules. |
| BR-104 | Notification preferences are user-specific. |
| BR-105 | Notification history shall be retained for auditing purposes. |
| BR-106 | Failed notifications shall follow retry policies before being marked as failed. |
| BR-107 | Every notification shall be traceable to its originating business event. |

# 7. Non-functional Considerations

## Performance

- Notification generation ≤ **2 seconds**
- Critical notification delivery ≤ **5 seconds**

---

## Availability

Notification service availability ≥ **99.9%**

---

## Scalability

Support:

- Millions of notifications per day
- Multiple delivery channels
- Multiple factories
- High concurrent notification delivery

---

## Security

- Notifications must respect factory-level RBAC.
- Sensitive information shall not be delivered to unauthorized recipients.

---

## Reliability

- Guaranteed delivery where supported.
- Automatic retry mechanism.
- Delivery status tracking.
- Idempotent notification processing.
