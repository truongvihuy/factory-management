# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the functional requirements for the **Incident Management** module.

The Incident Management module is responsible for recording, assigning, tracking, resolving, verifying, and closing machine-related incidents.

The module ensures that every operational issue is traceable, measurable, and resolved within the defined Service Level Agreement (SLA).

---

# 2. Business Scope

The module supports:

- Incident Registration
- Incident Assignment
- Incident Prioritization
- SLA Management
- Incident Investigation
- Incident Resolution
- Incident Verification
- Incident Closure
- Major Incident Management
- Incident Search
- Incident Dashboard
- Incident Reporting
- Audit Logging

---

# 3. Incident Lifecycle

```text
Open
   │
Assigned
   │
In Progress
   │
Resolved
   │
Verified
   │
Closed
```

An incident may also be **Cancelled** before work begins.

---

# 4. Business Rules

- One Incident shall be associated with one Machine.
- One Machine may have multiple Incidents.
- Multiple Incidents caused by the same root cause may be grouped under one Major Incident.
- Closed Incidents are read-only.
- Historical Incident records shall never be deleted.
- SLA shall be monitored continuously.

---

# 5. Incident Priority

| Priority | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Low | Minor issue with minimal operational impact |
| Medium | Operational issue requiring attention |
| High | Serious issue affecting production |
| Critical | Immediate action required |

# 6. Functional Requirements

---

# FR-INC-001 Create Incident

## Description

The system shall allow authorized users to create an incident manually.

### Actors

- Operator
- Maintenance Engineer
- Factory Manager

### Required Information

- Machine
- Incident Title
- Incident Category
- Priority
- Description

---

# FR-INC-002 Auto Create Incident

The system may automatically create incidents from Critical Alerts based on configurable business rules.

Automatically created incidents shall reference the originating alert.

---

# FR-INC-003 Update Incident

Authorized users may update incident information before closure.

Editable fields include:

- Description
- Priority
- Assigned Engineer
- Estimated Resolution Time

Audit logs shall be recorded.

---

# FR-INC-004 Assign Incident

Maintenance Supervisors shall assign incidents to Maintenance Engineers.

Assignment history shall be preserved.

---

# FR-INC-005 Change Incident Status

Supported statuses:

- Open
- Assigned
- In Progress
- Resolved
- Verified
- Closed
- Cancelled

Status transitions shall follow the defined Incident Lifecycle.

---

# FR-INC-006 SLA Monitoring

The system shall monitor SLA for every incident.

Example SLA:

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Time |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Critical | 15 Minutes | 1 Hour |
| High | 30 Minutes | 4 Hours |
| Medium | 2 Hours | 8 Hours |
| Low | 4 Hours | 24 Hours |

SLA values shall be configurable.

---

# FR-INC-007 SLA Violation

The system shall detect SLA violations.

When an SLA is exceeded:

- Notify responsible users.
- Escalate to higher-level management.
- Record the violation.

---

# FR-INC-008 Incident Investigation

The assigned engineer shall record investigation details.

Information includes:

- Root Cause
- Investigation Notes
- Findings
- Attachments (Optional)

---

# FR-INC-009 Resolve Incident

Maintenance Engineers shall resolve incidents.

Resolution information includes:

- Resolution Summary
- Corrective Action
- Resolution Time
- Spare Parts Used (Optional)

---

# FR-INC-010 Verify Incident

Maintenance Supervisors shall verify incident resolution.

Verification includes:

- Verification Result
- Verification Comment
- Verification Time

---

# FR-INC-011 Close Incident

Verified incidents may be closed.

Closed incidents become read-only.

---

# FR-INC-012 Cancel Incident

Authorized users may cancel incidents before work begins.

Cancellation shall require:

- Cancellation Reason
- Cancelled By
- Cancellation Time

---

# FR-INC-013 Major Incident Management

The system shall support grouping related incidents under one Major Incident.

Each child incident shall maintain its own lifecycle while remaining linked to the parent Major Incident.

---

# FR-INC-014 View Incident

Display:

- Incident Information
- Machine Information
- Alert Information
- SLA Status
- Assigned Engineer
- Timeline
- Investigation
- Resolution

---

# FR-INC-015 Search Incident

Support searching by:

- Incident ID
- Machine
- Factory
- Workshop
- Production Line
- Priority
- Status
- Assigned Engineer
- Time Range

Support sorting and pagination.

---

# FR-INC-016 Incident Timeline

Display the complete timeline:

- Created
- Assigned
- Started
- SLA Events
- Resolution
- Verification
- Closure

---

# FR-INC-017 Incident Dashboard

Display:

- Open Incidents
- Incidents by Priority
- SLA Violations
- Incidents by Factory
- Incidents by Machine
- MTTR
- MTBF

Dashboard shall update in near real time.

---

# FR-INC-018 Incident Export

Authorized users shall export incident records.

Supported formats:

- Excel
- PDF
- CSV

---

# FR-INC-019 Incident Archive

Closed incidents shall remain searchable.

Archived incidents shall preserve complete historical information.

---

# FR-INC-020 Audit Logging

The system shall record:

- Incident Created
- Incident Updated
- Assignment Changed
- Status Changed
- SLA Violation
- Investigation Updated
- Incident Resolved
- Incident Verified
- Incident Closed
- Incident Cancelled

Each audit record shall contain:

- User
- Timestamp
- Action
- Previous Value
- New Value

# 8. Non-functional Considerations

## Performance

- Incident creation shall complete within **3 seconds**.
- Incident search should complete within **2 seconds**.
- SLA monitoring shall run continuously.

---

## Reliability

- SLA timers shall continue operating after system restart.
- Incident history shall remain immutable.
- Timeline events shall never be lost.

---

## Security

- Only authorized users may modify incidents.
- Verification shall only be performed by authorized supervisors.
- All incident activities shall be audited.

---

## Data Integrity

- One Incident belongs to one Machine.
- Closed incidents cannot be modified.
- Historical records shall never be physically deleted.
